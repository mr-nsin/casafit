Crunches burn belly fat

What is true

Your body takes fat from all over at once. You cannot pick where it comes from by training one area. Crunches make your stomach muscles stronger, but they do not remove the fat on top.

science
What the research found. In a six-week trial, abdominal training did not change belly fat. Fat loss came from the whole-body picture: movement, food and sleep.
