Rest is part of training

Muscles do not get stronger while you work. They get stronger afterwards, while you rest. A rest day is when the work you did turns into progress.

That is why your plan has a rest or walk day between harder days. You can still move: a gentle walk counts.

lightbulbKey point. Feeling a little stiff is normal. Sharp pain is not. If something hurts, stop and tell us.
